PRIVACY · ANGLE DARE 1.0.4

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 Controller: Baripas Studio, Türkiye Contact: contact@baripas.studio

Angle Dare is a single-player precision game. Core play works offline and does not require an account. This policy explains what the app and its service providers process.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores game progress locally: Gold, completed rounds and results, Daily Five progress, challenge codes you create, equipped cosmetics, settings, language, tutorial completion, and Premium status. This data stays on the device unless you use an operating-system backup or the Android cloud-save feature.

You can remove local data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. Store purchases can be restored after reinstall by using Restore Purchases.

2. Motion and sensor data

During a round the app reads the device's rotation-vector/motion sensor to calculate the angle. This processing happens on the device in real time. The app does not record, upload, or sell raw sensor data.

3. Purchases

Monthly Premium and the one-time lifetime unlock are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The app receives product information, a transaction identifier, and entitlement status. It does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple/Google handle billing, taxes, refunds, and renewal or cancellation under their own policies.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads and Liftoff (Vungle) demand. Those advertising SDKs may process device information, advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address, app version, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

The current iOS build does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt and declares no tracking in its app privacy manifest. Consent handling is fail-closed for personalized advertising in regions where consent is required. Premium removes ads and suppresses ad display; subsequent launches skip ad initialization.

See the providers' policies:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff: https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/
  • IronSource/LevelPlay: https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/

5. Sharing

Result cards are generated locally. When you tap Share, the operating-system share sheet lets you choose a destination. The app does not upload the card to its own server. The receiving app processes it under that app's policy.

6. Analytics, accounts, and cloud

The release build contains no first-party analytics backend and no account system. On Android, the optional Play Games cloud-save feature may sign in to Google Play Games and store your game state with Google; on iOS this feature is unavailable and local storage is used. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise location access.

7. Children

Angle Dare is a general-audience game rated 4+. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can address it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the app has no account, most data is controlled on your device; use the app's clear-data/uninstall controls and the store's purchase tools. For requests, contact contact@baripas.studio. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. International transfers

Advertising and store providers may process data in countries other than yours under their safeguards. The app itself does not operate a server or transfer local game data internationally.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or law changes. The effective date will be revised and the new version published at the privacy URL before it applies.
